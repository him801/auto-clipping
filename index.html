Auto Clipping

A personal, single-user tool that turns my own PS5 gameplay livestreams into short highlight clips for my own YouTube channel.

What it does

  1. Finds new recordings. It checks my own channel for livestream recordings I made from my PS5.
  2. Suggests highlights. It suggests funny or exciting moments, with Cantonese subtitles and title ideas.
  3. I review everything. Nothing is published without my manual approval of each clip.
  4. Uploads approved clips. Approved clips are uploaded to my own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), with the title, description and publish time I approved.

Who can use it

Auto Clipping is not a public service. It runs only on my own computer and is used only by me, the owner of the YouTube channel it uploads to. It does not access, collect or upload anything for other YouTube users.
